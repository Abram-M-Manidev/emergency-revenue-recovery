## The service
ESSR answers calls for service businesses, collects caller details, offers
and books appointment times from the business's own schedule, records
emergency requests as tickets, and can alert contacts the business chooses.

## Not an emergency service (must survive review in substance)
ESSR is not a substitute for emergency services. It cannot contact police,
fire, ambulance or gas emergency lines, and does not guarantee that a
technician will attend. When a caller reports a hazard such as a gas smell,
smoke or a carbon monoxide alarm, ESSR tells them to get to safety and call
the emergency number. Alerts depend on channels the business configures and
on third-party networks, and may be delayed or fail.

## AI limitations
Automated speech recognition and AI can mishear, misunderstand or make
mistakes. Businesses remain responsible for reviewing tickets and
appointments, their services and pricing, and their response to customers.

## No performance guarantees
No promise of any increase in bookings or revenue.
