# Legal and privacy drafts

**Status: DRAFTS. None of these documents has been reviewed by a lawyer.
Nothing here is legal advice or a statement that ESSR complies with any law.**
They exist so a qualified lawyer can review and finish them quickly, and so
the product's actual behaviour is described accurately to that lawyer.

ESSR is operated from India and will process data about callers and staff
of service businesses in other countries (initially the US and Canada). The
documents below are written for that situation and flag every decision a
lawyer must make.

| Document | Purpose | Blocking for paid pilot? |
|---|---|---|
| `PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md` | Public privacy notice (website + service) | Yes |
| `TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md` | Public terms; emergency disclaimer | Yes |
| `DPA_DRAFT.md` | Data processing terms with each business | Yes |
| `SUBPROCESSORS.md` | Who processes data for ESSR, where | Yes (DPA annex) |
| `DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md` | What is kept, how long, how deleted | Yes |
| `SECURITY_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` | What happens on a breach | Yes |
| `LEGAL_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` | Every open legal question, by jurisdiction | Read first |

The website pages `/privacy` and `/terms` mirror the first two drafts and
show a visible "Draft pending legal review" notice. Remove that notice only
after review, and keep the page text and these files in step.
